# RESOLVE: Case Zero
A text-based horror-action game made in Python showcasing a sliver of the world of RESOLVE.

# About
You play as Adron Etere, an Anomaly Response Technician for Royce Technologies. You and your team must work together to solve a mystery haunting a local family. However, as you progress through the case, you must keep fighting as your own will starts to slip out of your hands.

# How to Run
1. Download the RESOLVE-Case-Zero.py file and save it to a folder of your choosing
2. Open your terminal
3. Enter -> cd /path/to/your/folder
4. If you're on Windows, enter in "python RESOLVE-Case-Zero.py". If you're on Mac, enter in "python3 RESOLVE-Case-Zero.py".
5. Enjoy your first taste of the world of RESOLVE

# Gameplay
To make a choice, type in the number corresponding to the choice (just the digit, nothing else) and press enter.

Explore the house and inspect different items to piece together the mystery. Fight and exorcise anomalies within the house. Make sure to avoid depleting your BODY or burning out your NERVE.
